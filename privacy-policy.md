Privacy Policy — Bible Memory

Bible Memory respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app uses, where it is stored, and when data may be sent to third-party services.

Information We Collect

Bible Memory does not require account registration and does not collect personal profile information directly.

Depending on how you use the app, the following data may be used:

- Verse data you add or edit, including Bible version, reference, verse text, tags, difficulty, and review state
- Progress data such as review history, pass counts, mastery, streaks, and scheduling information
- Voice recordings you choose to create for verses
- Speech recognition input used while reviewing verses or dictating verse text
- Optional OpenAI API key if you enable AI mode

How Data Is Stored

- Verse data, settings, and progress data are stored locally on your device
- Recorded verse audio files are stored locally on your device
- Your OpenAI API key is stored locally in the iOS Keychain

Microphone And Speech Recognition

Bible Memory requests microphone and speech recognition access only for app features that need voice input, such as:

- reciting verses in Review Session
- dictating verse text in Add Verse or Edit Verse
- recording verse audio for playback

Speech recognition is provided through Apple system frameworks. Audio used for speech recognition may be processed by Apple, depending on device capabilities and system behavior.

AI (OpenAI) Mode

Bible Memory includes an optional AI mode that is off by default.

If you enable AI mode and provide your own OpenAI API key:

- the app may send verse lookup or validation requests to OpenAI
- the content you submit for those AI features may be transmitted to OpenAI
- your OpenAI API key is stored locally on your device in Keychain and is not shared by the app except when making your OpenAI requests

If AI mode is disabled, Bible Memory does not send AI requests to OpenAI.

Third-Party Services

Bible Memory does not include third-party advertising SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs.

The app may use these platform or service providers when you use certain features:

- Apple speech recognition and audio services for microphone and speech features
- OpenAI, only if you enable AI mode and use AI-related features

Data Sharing

Bible Memory does not sell your personal data.

The app does not share your data with third parties except as needed to provide optional features you choose to use, such as Apple speech recognition or OpenAI AI requests.

Data Retention And Control

You control the content you create in the app.

- You can edit or delete verses in the app
- You can reset progress data in the app
- You can disable AI mode at any time
- You can remove the app to delete locally stored app data from your device
